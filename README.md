## 🎮 About the Game

Iron Blight is a single-player survival horror and action game developed by Ronesans Interactive and released on August 24, 2026. Set in a cold, rain-soaked Eastern Bloc town, the game follows a survivor searching for his wife, Valeria, while facing abominations, scarce supplies, environmental puzzles, and oppressive isolation.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Iron Blight**. This trainer provides optional assistance for Iron Blight's survival-horror systems, including health, ammunition, weapon handling, resources, inventory space, and movement. It is designed to reduce resource pressure while preserving the game's exploration, puzzle-solving, and combat structure.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to reduce resource scarcity, practice combat, explore Prasnova more freely, revisit the story, or make the game's survival systems less demanding.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents the player from losing health during encounters and exploration.
- **Unlimited Food** — Prevents food supplies from being consumed during survival gameplay.

- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Keeps ammunition available for firearms without removing the need to aim and manage engagements.
- **No Reload** — Allows weapons to fire without the usual step-by-step reload process.
- **No Weapon Durability Loss** — Prevents supported weapons and equipment from degrading during use.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Stops hostile creatures from moving while the option is active.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Super Movement** — Improves player movement speed for faster traversal through streets and interiors.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Iron Blight uses first-person exploration across desolate streets and claustrophobic interiors. Move carefully, inspect the environment, and use the movement option only when faster traversal is preferable to the intended pace. Avoid activating movement enhancements during scripted sequences or narrow stairways.

### Tools & Inventory

The game uses a limited diegetic grid-based inventory, making item placement and resource choices important. Use expanded capacity when you want to carry more food, medical supplies, ammunition, tools, and quest items. Keep essential tools and healing supplies available before entering unfamiliar areas.

### World & Environment

Explore the rain-soaked town of Prasnova, search rooms and streets for supplies, collect tools, solve environmental puzzles, and investigate clues connected to Valeria. The trainer can reduce the pressure caused by scarce resources, but puzzle progression and key objectives still require normal exploration.

### Combat & Weapons

Every bullet matters in the standard experience, and gunfire can attract additional horrors. Weapons use deliberate manual inspection and reload actions. Use unlimited ammunition or no reload for a more accessible combat experience, and use freeze enemies or stealth mode when learning enemy behavior or avoiding difficult encounters.
